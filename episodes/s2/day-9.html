IPTV Starts Buffering When Someone Else Uses the Internet

Part 1 of 5

The moment someone starts downloading, your show starts spinning.

You're watching TV without a problem. Someone else starts a large download, joins a video call, or begins streaming another video. Suddenly your IPTV begins buffering.

Part 2 of 5

Try a simple household experiment

Pick a time when the problem normally happens.

First, watch the affected service while other devices are doing very little online. Note whether playback is stable.

Then, if practical, have another household device perform its normal activity again. Does the buffering return?

Part 3 of 5

Find out what is using the connection

Large game downloads, cloud backups, video calls, and multiple high-quality video streams can consume network capacity.

If your router provides a connected-device list or traffic information, use its official instructions to see what is connected. Not every router offers the same tools.

Part 4 of 5

Test the TV's connection

If you're using Wi-Fi, check the signal where the TV is located. A phone beside the router can have a very different connection from a television behind walls and furniture.

If your device supports Ethernet, testing a wired connection may help determine whether Wi-Fi is part of the problem.

Part 5 of 5

Don't buy a faster internet plan just yet

A faster plan may help if your connection's capacity is genuinely insufficient, but it won't automatically fix every cause of buffering.

If only one service fails while other online activities work normally, the issue could also be with the app or provider.
