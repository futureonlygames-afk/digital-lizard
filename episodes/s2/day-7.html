Your Remote Works Everywhere Except in IPTV

Part 1 of 4

The remote is fine. The app is the one being difficult.

You can move around the TV's home screen. You can open settings. Another app works normally. Then you open IPTV and one or more buttons stop responding.

Part 2 of 4

A quick three-part test

Test one: the TV menu. Use the directional buttons and select button in the normal TV settings. Do they respond? Test two: another app. Open a different application and navigate its menus. If the buttons work there, the remote is less likely to be the problem. Test three: the IPTV app. Check whether all buttons fail or only certain actions. For example, navigation might work while the playback controls do not.

Part 3 of 4

What should you try?

Close and reopen the IPTV app. If that doesn't help, restart the TV or streaming device. Check whether the app has an official update and whether its documentation mentions remote-control compatibility. If you use an external streaming box, confirm that you're controlling the correct device. Some setups use separate controls for the TV and streaming box, and their functions can overlap. If the remote also fails in other menus, check the batteries, pairing, and manufacturer's troubleshooting instructions.

Part 4 of 4

When should you contact support?

If the remote works everywhere else but consistently fails in one application, report that exact behavior to the app developer. Include the device model and which buttons are affected.
