IPTV Playing the Wrong Language? Check This Before Changing Everything

Part 1 of 5

You selected a movie. The movie selected a different language.

Sometimes when you press play expecting English, the dialogue starts in French, Spanish, Arabic, or another language you weren't expecting.

Part 2 of 5

Look inside the player first

Start playing the program. Open the playback controls and look for an option labelled Audio , Language , or Audio track . The exact wording depends on the application.

If several tracks are listed, choose the one you want and see whether the dialogue changes.

Part 3 of 5

What if the app keeps choosing the wrong language?

Check whether your application has a preferred audio-language setting. Some players allow you to choose a default language; others leave the choice to each program.

It's also worth checking the device's language and regional settings, especially if the problem started after someone changed the TV configuration.

Part 4 of 5

Run a small test

Try another program that you know has multiple audio options.

If the second program offers the language you want, the first program may simply lack that track.

Part 5 of 5

A note about subtitles

Subtitles are not the same as an audio track. If you want to hear the original language but read translations, look for subtitles instead. Whether this is possible depends on the content and player.
