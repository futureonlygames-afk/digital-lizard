IPTV Keeps Dumping You Back to the Home Screen

Part 1 of 4

You're halfway through a program, then suddenly you're staring at the home screen.

No warning. No useful explanation. Just the TV's main menu. This is different from buffering. Buffering interrupts playback while an app is still running. If the application closes entirely, start by investigating the app and device.

Part 2 of 4

First question: is it just this app?

Open another streaming app and use it for a few minutes. If other apps also close unexpectedly, the problem may involve the TV or streaming device rather than one specific IPTV app. If only one app closes, focus your investigation there.

Part 3 of 4

Try this sequence

Restart the device. Use the normal restart option when available. On some devices, turning the screen off only puts the TV into standby, so follow the manufacturer's instructions for a proper restart. Check for updates. Install official updates for the IPTV app and the device's operating system when available. Look at available storage. If your device is almost full, remove apps you no longer use. Google provides instructions for managing storage on Android TV devices. Close unnecessary background apps. Some devices allow you to force-stop applications that are still running. Follow the instructions for your model rather than assuming every TV has the same menus. Check for overheating. If an external streaming box feels unusually hot, make sure its ventilation openings aren't blocked and that it has room to release heat.

Part 4 of 4

What if it still happens?

Record when the app closes. Does it happen immediately, only when playing one program, or after a similar amount of time each session? That pattern may help support distinguish a content-specific issue from an app crash or device problem. Reinstalling the app can be a later step, but make sure you understand how to sign back in first. A factory reset is more disruptive and should not be your first response.
