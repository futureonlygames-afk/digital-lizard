IPTV Favorites Disappeared? Here's How to Investigate

Part 1 of 5

You finally organized your favorites. Now they're gone.

You picked the channels you actually watch, put them in a sensible order, and stopped scrolling through a giant list.

Then you open the app the next day and discover that your favorites have vanished.

Before recreating the entire list, try to work out what changed. Favorites may be saved locally on the device, associated with an app profile, or handled differently depending on the application. There isn't one universal storage method for every IPTV player.

Part 2 of 5

Did you open the same profile?

If your app or device supports multiple profiles, make sure you're using the same one as before.

This is an easy detail to overlook, especially in a household where several people use the same TV.

Part 3 of 5

Did anything change recently?

Think back. Did you reinstall the app, clear its data, switch profiles, update the application, or reset the device?

These details matter because clearing app data or reinstalling an application can remove locally stored preferences. Google's Google TV troubleshooting guide also warns that clearing app data is a troubleshooting step that should be considered carefully.

Part 4 of 5

What to do next

Check the profile. Switch back to the profile where your favorites were originally saved, if applicable.

Look for a favorites or synchronization setting. Consult the app's official documentation to find out whether favorites are stored locally or synchronized.

Restart the app. If the list is present but isn't displaying correctly, a fresh launch may help.

Check for updates. If the problem appeared after an update, look for a later official version or support notice.

Contact app support if the list should sync. Explain when the favorites disappeared and whether other settings were affected.

A factory reset is not a sensible first step for a missing favorites list. It can remove much more than the information you're trying to recover.

Part 5 of 5

How to avoid losing favorites again

If your app offers an official backup or synchronization feature, find out how it works and whether it is enabled. Don't assume every app stores favorites in the cloud.

Digital Lizard takeawayBefore rebuilding your favorites, check the profile and the app's storage behavior. You might be looking in the wrong place, or the settings may genuinely have been removed.
