IPTV Picture Is Zoomed In or Cropped? Here's How to Fix It

Part 1 of 6

Why is the scoreboard missing half its numbers?

You're watching a match. The picture looks too large, the text near the edges is missing, and the channel logo is half cut off.

Part 2 of 6

Start with a simple comparison

Open another channel or program. If everything looks cropped, the issue may be in the TV's picture settings or the app's video player.

If only one program looks wrong, the source itself may be using a different format.

Part 3 of 6

Check the player first

While the video is playing, look for a setting called Aspect ratio, Screen mode, Zoom, Fit to screen, or something similar.

The available options vary by app. If there's a normal, original, or automatic display option, try it and check whether the full image returns.

Part 4 of 6

Then check your TV

Your television may have a separate picture-size control. Open its picture settings and look for an option that displays the full image rather than zooming in.

Don't worry if the menu uses different wording from the examples above. Manufacturers organize these settings differently.

Part 5 of 6

What if you're using a streaming box?

The external device may have its own display configuration. Check its official display settings and make sure they are suitable for your TV.

Change one setting at a time. If the image gets worse, restore the previous setting before trying another.

Part 6 of 6

When the picture is still cropped

If only one app has the problem, check its documentation or contact the developer. If all apps and menus appear cropped, consult your TV manufacturer's support guidance.
