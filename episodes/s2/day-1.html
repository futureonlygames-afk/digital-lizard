IPTV Guide Not Showing? How to Fix Missing Program Information

Part 1 of 4

The channels work, but where did the TV guide go?

You open your TV app to see what's on tonight. The channels are there, the video plays, but the program guide looks like an abandoned calendar. No show titles. No descriptions. Nothing to help you decide what to watch.

Part 2 of 4

First, check the pattern

Try three or four channels. Are all their program descriptions missing, or is it just one or two?

Part 3 of 4

Try these fixes in order

  1. Close and reopen the app. Don't just return to the home screen if the app continues running in the background. Close it fully if your device offers that option, then reopen it.
  2. Check your device's date and time. A badly incorrect clock can cause problems with time-sensitive information in some applications. Enable automatic date and time if your device supports it.
  3. Look for a guide refresh option. Some applications provide a way to refresh program information. Use the option documented by the app, if available. Not every app has one.
  4. Check for an official update. Look in your device's official app store for an update to the application. Avoid unofficial modified versions.
  5. Ask the provider if the problem remains. Explain whether the channels play normally and whether the guide is empty across the entire lineup. That is much more useful than simply reporting that IPTV is broken.

Part 4 of 4

What if the guide is still empty?

If the provider confirms that schedule information should be available, give support the name of your app, device model, and a couple of affected channels. You do not need to send your password.
